Before publishing, confirm the content matches the app's actual behavior and adjust the contact and effective date as needed. ## 1. What data we collect This app is an offline-first track recorder. We **operate no servers, require no account, and collect no personal identity data**. The app processes the following on your device: - **Location data** (precise and approximate): to show your position on the map, record movement tracks, and reveal the "exploration fog". - **Track data**: routes, timestamps, speed, and elevation recorded in standard GPX format. - **Fog exploration data**: the set of map cells you have visited. All of the above is **stored only in the app's private sandbox on your device** and, by default, never leaves your device. ## 2. Background location The app uses background location in two cases, both **initiated by you, shown with a persistent notification while running, and with data stored only on your device**: - **Manual recording**: once you start recording a track, the app keeps obtaining your location in the background — even when the screen is off or you switch to another app — so the route is recorded completely. It runs only while a recording is in progress and stops when you stop. - **Auto-record mode (opt-in)**: when you enable it, the app keeps locating in the background to detect your movement — it auto-starts recording when you move and auto-saves after you stay still. This mode uses the `LOCATION_IN_BACKGROUND` permission and the `KEEP_BACKGROUND_RUNNING` long-running task, and requires you to grant location "Allow all the time" in system settings. You can turn it off in the app at any time, which stops background location. The app **does not collect location in the background unless you have started it** (by starting a recording or enabling auto-record mode). ## 3. Network use The app uses the network (the `INTERNET` permission) only to: - load map tiles (public map providers such as OpenStreetMap, Esri, CARTO, OpenSeaMap); - query RainViewer (rain-radar tiles) and Open-Meteo (current weather) to show weather; the weather query sends your approximate coordinates (rounded to 2 decimals, ~1 km); - after a recording finishes and when you view the Timeline, reverse-geocode the track's start/end points and each day's stop points via the system location service to auto-label tracks (e.g. "Place A → Place B") and show visited places on the Timeline, for display only (falls back to coordinates or the timestamp filename if no result is found); - **Place recognition (POI, off by default)**: once you turn on "Place recognition" in Settings, ending a recording sends that session's stop-point coordinates to the OSM volunteer-run Overpass API to look up nearby named places (e.g. shops, landmarks) for the Timeline; no such query is made while this is off. You can also manually trigger the same lookup for any stop from the Timeline's "Edit location" feature, independent of this setting. Place names found are stored only on your device; - transfer data when you actively perform a cloud / WebDAV backup or restore. Map tile requests go to the respective map providers and are subject to their own privacy policies. ## 4. Backup and cloud - Backups are **triggered entirely by you**. - You may export to a local file, WebDAV (Nextcloud / Nutstore / NAS), Huawei Cloud, or the app-specific folder of your own Dropbox account. - Data is written only to **a location you choose / your own account**, which we cannot access. - Cloud authorization uses OAuth2, scoped to that app-specific folder only. ## 5. What we do not do - No ads, no behavioral analytics, no tracking. - We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. - We do not store any of your data on our servers (we have none). ## 6. Data deletion You can delete individual tracks in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all local data from your device. For backups you uploaded to a cloud / WebDAV service yourself, please delete them within that service. ## 7.
