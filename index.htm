隱私權政策 — 謎之迷霧 mizu fog world

生效日期：2026-06-24　·　套件名：com.mizufog.info　·　聯絡：mizuhara@mshw.info

1. 我們蒐集哪些資料

本 App 是離線優先的軌跡記錄工具。我們不經營伺服器、不設帳號、不蒐集個人身分資料。App 在你的裝置上處理以下資料：

  • 位置資訊（精確與概略定位）：用於在地圖上顯示你的位置、記錄移動軌跡，以及揭開「探索迷霧」。
  • 軌跡資料：以標準 GPX 格式記錄的路徑、時間、速度、海拔等。
  • 迷霧探索資料：你走過的地理格子集合。

以上資料僅儲存在你裝置的 App 私有沙箱，預設不會離開你的裝置。

2. 背景定位

當你開始記錄軌跡後，即使螢幕關閉或切換到其他 App，本 App 仍會在背景持續取得位置以完整記錄路徑。背景定位僅在記錄進行中運作，停止記錄即結束。

3. 網路使用

本 App 使用網路僅為：

  • 載入地圖圖磚（OpenStreetMap、Esri、CARTO、OpenSeaMap 等公開圖資供應商）；
  • 顯示天氣時向 RainViewer（雨勢雷達圖磚）與 Open-Meteo（即時天氣）查詢；查詢天氣會送出你目前位置的概略座標（取至小數點後兩位，約 1 公里精度）；
  • 你主動操作雲端備份 / WebDAV 上傳還原時的資料傳輸。

上述第三方服務各自適用其隱私政策。

4. 備份與雲端

  • 備份完全由你主動觸發。
  • 你可選擇匯出到本機檔案、WebDAV、華為雲空間，或你自己的 OneDrive／Dropbox／Google Drive 帳號的 App 專屬資料夾。
  • 資料只會寫入你自行選擇的位置 / 你自己的帳號，我們無法存取。
  • 雲端授權採 OAuth2，授權範圍限縮於該 App 專屬資料夾。

5. 我們不做的事

  • 不投放廣告、不做行為分析或追蹤。
  • 不將你的資料出售、出租或分享給第三方。
  • 不在我們的伺服器上儲存你的任何資料（我們沒有伺服器）。

6. 資料刪除

你可隨時在 App 內刪除個別軌跡，或解除安裝 App 以移除裝置上的所有本地資料。你自行上傳到雲端／WebDAV 的備份檔，請於該服務中自行刪除。

7. 兒童

本 App 非針對兒童設計，亦不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

8. 政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會於本頁更新生效日期。

9. 聯絡我們

Privacy Policy — mizu fog world

Effective date: 2026-06-24　·　Bundle: com.mizufog.info　·　Contact: mizuhara@mshw.info

1. What data we collect

This app is an offline-first track recorder. We operate no servers, require no account, and collect no personal identity data. The app processes the following on your device:

  • Location data (precise and approximate): to show your position on the map, record movement tracks, and reveal the "exploration fog".
  • Track data: routes, timestamps, speed, and elevation recorded in standard GPX format.
  • Fog exploration data: the set of map cells you have visited.

All of the above is stored only in the app's private sandbox on your device and, by default, never leaves your device.

2. Background location

Once you start recording a track, the app continues to obtain your location in the background — even when the screen is off or you switch to another app — so the route is recorded completely. Background location runs only while a recording is in progress and stops when you stop recording.

3. Network use

The app uses the network only to:

  • load map tiles (public providers such as OpenStreetMap, Esri, CARTO, OpenSeaMap);
  • query RainViewer (rain-radar tiles) and Open-Meteo (current weather) to show weather; the weather query sends your approximate coordinates (rounded to 2 decimals, ~1 km);
  • transfer data when you actively perform a cloud / WebDAV backup or restore.

These third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Backup and cloud

  • Backups are triggered entirely by you.
  • You may export to a local file, WebDAV, Huawei Cloud, or the app-specific folder of your own OneDrive / Dropbox / Google Drive account.
  • Data is written only to a location you choose / your own account, which we cannot access.
  • Cloud authorization uses OAuth2, scoped to that app-specific folder only.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads, no behavioral analytics, no tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not store any of your data on our servers (we have none).

6. Data deletion

You can delete individual tracks in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. For backups you uploaded to a cloud / WebDAV service yourself, please delete them within that service.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date on this page.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: mizuhara@mshw.info
