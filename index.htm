隱私權政策 — 謎之迷霧 mizu fog world

生效日期：2026-09-08　·　套件名：com.mizufog.info　·　聯絡：mizuhara@mshw.info

1. 我們蒐集哪些資料

本 App 是離線優先的軌跡記錄工具。我們不經營伺服器、不設帳號、不蒐集個人身分資料。App 在你的裝置上處理以下資料：

  • 位置資訊（精確與概略定位）：用於在地圖上顯示你的位置、記錄移動軌跡，以及揭開「探索迷霧」。
  • 軌跡資料：以標準 GPX 格式記錄的路徑、時間、速度、海拔等。
  • 迷霧探索資料：你走過的地理格子集合。
  • 停留點照片（選填）：你可在時間軸的任一停留點手動選擇相簿中的照片掛上去；App 會壓縮成縮圖複製一份存進私有沙箱，不會保留原相簿的照片路徑，因此原照片被刪除或換手機都不影響已掛上的照片。此功能完全選填、不會自動運作，且不會透過網路傳輸、不納入現有的備份匯出。

以上資料僅儲存在你裝置的 App 私有沙箱，預設不會離開你的裝置。

2. 背景定位

本 App 有兩種會用到背景定位的情形，皆由你主動啟動、執行期間顯示常駐通知，且資料僅儲存在你的裝置：

  • 手動記錄：當你開始記錄軌跡後，即使螢幕關閉或切換到其他 App，仍會在背景持續取得位置以完整記錄路徑。背景定位僅在記錄進行中運作，停止記錄即結束。
  • 自動記錄模式（需你主動開啟）：開啟後，App 會在背景持續定位以偵測你的移動——偵測到移動自動開始記錄、靜止一段時間自動結束並存檔。此模式使用 LOCATION_IN_BACKGROUND 權限與 KEEP_BACKGROUND_RUNNING 長時任務，需你在系統設定中將定位授權為「始終允許」；你可隨時在 App 內關閉，關閉後即停止背景定位。

本 App 不會在你未啟動（按下記錄或開啟自動模式）的情況下於背景蒐集位置。

3. 網路使用

本 App 使用網路僅為：

  • 載入地圖圖磚（Esri、OpenSeaMap 等公開圖資供應商）；
  • 顯示天氣時向 RainViewer（雨勢雷達圖磚）、Open-Meteo（即時天氣）與 Open-Meteo 空氣品質 API（AQI）查詢；查詢會送出你目前位置的概略座標（取至小數點後兩位，約 1 公里精度）；
  • 記錄結束後與檢視時間軸時，透過系統定位服務反查軌跡起訖點／當天各停留點的地點名稱，用來自動命名軌跡（如「某地 → 某地」）與顯示時間軸的造訪地點（僅供顯示用；查無結果時保留原本座標或時間戳記檔名）；
  • 地點辨識（POI，預設關閉）：在設定頁開啟「地點辨識」後，每次記錄結束會把該次記錄的停留點座標送到 OSM 志工維運的 Overpass API，查詢附近具名地點（例如店家、地標），用於時間軸顯示；未開啟則不會進行此查詢。你也可以在時間軸「編輯地點」功能中，對任一停留點手動觸發一次同樣的查詢（不受此開關限制）。查到的地點名稱只會存放在你的裝置上；
  • 你主動操作雲端備份 / WebDAV 上傳還原時的資料傳輸。

上述第三方服務各自適用其隱私政策。

4. 備份與雲端

  • 備份完全由你主動觸發。
  • 你可選擇匯出到本機檔案、WebDAV（Nextcloud／堅果雲／NAS）、華為雲空間，或你自己的 Dropbox 帳號的 App 專屬資料夾。

  • 雲端授權採 OAuth2，授權範圍限縮於該 App 專屬資料夾。

5. 我們不做的事

  • 不投放廣告、不做行為分析或追蹤。
  • 不將你的資料出售、出租或分享給第三方。
  • 不在我們的伺服器上儲存你的任何資料（我們沒有伺服器）。

6. 資料刪除

你可隨時在 App 內刪除個別軌跡或個別照片，備份頁亦提供「清除全部照片」一鍵清除；解除安裝 App 會移除裝置上的所有本地資料。你自行上傳到雲端 / WebDAV 的備份檔，請於該服務中自行刪除。

7. 兒童

本 App 非針對兒童設計，亦不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

Privacy Policy — mizu fog world

Effective date: 2026-09-08　·　Bundle: com.mizufog.info　·　Contact: mizuhara@mshw.info

1. What data we collect

This app is an offline-first track recorder. We operate no servers, require no account, and collect no personal identity data. The app processes the following on your device:

  • Location data (precise and approximate): to show your position on the map, record movement tracks, and reveal the "exploration fog".
  • Track data: routes, timestamps, speed, and elevation recorded in standard GPX format.
  • Fog exploration data: the set of map cells you have visited.
  • Stop-point photos (optional): you may manually attach photos from your gallery to any stop on the Timeline; the app compresses them into a thumbnail copy stored in its private sandbox — it does not keep a reference to the original photo, so the app's copy is unaffected if the original is deleted or you switch phones. This feature is entirely opt-in, never runs automatically, never involves network transfer, and is not included in the existing backup export.

All of the above is stored only in the app's private sandbox on your device and, by default, never leaves your device.

2. Background location

The app uses background location in two cases, both initiated by you, shown with a persistent notification while running, and with data stored only on your device:

  • Manual recording: once you start recording a track, the app keeps obtaining your location in the background — even when the screen is off or you switch to another app — so the route is recorded completely. It runs only while a recording is in progress and stops when you stop.
  • Auto-record mode (opt-in): when you enable it, the app keeps locating in the background to detect your movement — it auto-starts recording when you move and auto-saves after you stay still. This mode uses the LOCATION_IN_BACKGROUND permission and the KEEP_BACKGROUND_RUNNING long-running task, and requires you to grant location "Allow all the time" in system settings. You can turn it off in the app at any time, which stops background location.

The app does not collect location in the background unless you have started it (by starting a recording or enabling auto-record mode).

3. Network use

The app uses the network only to:

  • load map tiles (public providers such as Esri and OpenSeaMap);
  • query RainViewer (rain-radar tiles), Open-Meteo (current weather), and the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (AQI) to show weather; these queries send your approximate coordinates (rounded to 2 decimals, ~1 km);
  • after a recording finishes and when you view the Timeline, reverse-geocode the track's start/end points and each day's stop points via the system location service to auto-label tracks (e.g. "Place A → Place B") and show visited places on the Timeline, for display only (falls back to coordinates or the timestamp filename if no result is found);
  • Place recognition (POI, off by default): once you turn on "Place recognition" in Settings, ending a recording sends that session's stop-point coordinates to the OSM volunteer-run Overpass API to look up nearby named places (e.g. shops, landmarks) for the Timeline; no such query is made while this is off. You can also manually trigger the same lookup for any stop from the Timeline's "Edit location" feature, independent of this setting. Place names found are stored only on your device;
  • transfer data when you actively perform a cloud / WebDAV backup or restore.

These third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Backup and cloud

  • Backups are triggered entirely by you.
  • You may export to a local file, WebDAV (Nextcloud / Nutstore / NAS), Huawei Cloud, or the app-specific folder of your own Dropbox account.
  • Data is written only to a location you choose / your own account, which we cannot access.
  • Cloud authorization uses OAuth2, scoped to that app-specific folder only.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads, no behavioral analytics, no tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not store any of your data on our servers (we have none).

6. Data deletion

You can delete individual tracks or individual photos in the app at any time; the Backup page also offers a one-tap "Clear all photos". Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device. For backups you uploaded to a cloud / WebDAV service yourself, please delete them within that service.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
